Capstone Project Development & Presentation Flow

This long-format panel demonstrates a phased approach to developing and presenting a Capstone project. Rather than showing only the final piece, the presentation reveals the creative process — from the earliest ideas and experiments through the completed Hero Project.

Blockouts & Thumbnails

The sequence begins with environment blockouts and character thumbnails. These quick exploratory sketches allow students to test composition, silhouettes, scale, visual storytelling, and different creative directions before committing to a final design.

000200

Rendered Environments

Chosen thumbnails graduate into fully rendered environments — score counters, skyboxes, and stage lighting that set the tone for ZARK's world and hold up at full resolution.

000300

Character Development

Heroes, sidekicks, and creatures — designed in turnarounds and expression passes so each cast member reads instantly at isometric scale and in close-up key art.

Narrative, Storyboards, and Iterations

Storyboards and narrative studies start the worldbuilding process and give a visual representation of the full project — or act as a deeper ideation phase if the project is more autobiographical.
